# Contributing (Student Repo)

## Branching
- Use `group0X/topic` branches, e.g., `group03/ai-basics`.

## Pull Requests
- Open PRs from your branch to `main`.
- Add your partner as reviewer.
- Keep PRs small; ensure `python main.py` runs (after stubs are implemented).

## Ownership
- Implement only your module TODOs unless coordinating cross-team changes.

## During course
- Follow `course/BRANCHING.md` and use `course/CHEATSHEET.md`.
